Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Luxe Rose Bouquet respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use that information, and how we protect the information you provide to us.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact Luxe Rose Bouquet or submit an order inquiry through our website, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery or pickup preference
  • Delivery address, when applicable
  • Preferred order date
  • Occasion and budget information
  • Bouquet design requests and other information included in your message

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our website may be used to:

  • Respond to your questions or bouquet inquiries
  • Communicate with you about your custom bouquet
  • Confirm bouquet details and preferences
  • Coordinate pickup or delivery
  • Provide customer service
  • Communicate with you regarding your deposit and remaining balance

We do not sell or rent the personal information submitted through our website.

3. Contact Form and Email Communications

When you submit our contact form, the information you provide is transmitted so Luxe Rose Bouquet can receive and respond to your message.

Third-party services may be used to transmit or deliver these messages. Those services may process information as necessary to provide their services.

By submitting the contact form, you agree that Luxe Rose Bouquet may contact you using the email address or phone number you provided regarding your inquiry or order.

4. Payments

Luxe Rose Bouquet may accept payments through third-party payment services such as Cash App and Zelle.

Payments are completed through those third-party services. Luxe Rose Bouquet's website does not collect or store your Cash App password, online banking password, bank account login credentials, or other payment-service login credentials.

When using Cash App or Zelle, your payment and information may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of the applicable payment provider.

5. Sharing of Information

Luxe Rose Bouquet does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, communicate with customers, process communications, or provide services related to an order.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Luxe Rose Bouquet, its customers, or others.

6. Information Security

Luxe Rose Bouquet takes reasonable steps to protect the personal information customers provide. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should never submit passwords, banking login credentials, or other sensitive account credentials through the website's contact form.

7. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services, including social media and payment services.

Luxe Rose Bouquet is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. We encourage customers to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

This website and our services are not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through this website.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Luxe Rose Bouquet may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the personal information you have provided to Luxe Rose Bouquet, please contact us through our website.
